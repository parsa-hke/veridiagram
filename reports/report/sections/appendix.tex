\appendix
\section{Threshold 0.5 and the padding correction}
\label{sec:appendix}

Phase 7 evaluated each batch of 32 queries padded to its largest image, and because the pooled feature averages every patch token, padding included, a saved decision depended on its batch neighbours. Every checkpoint was re-evaluated with each query padded alone to a \inputLongSide{} px square; Table~\ref{tab:padding} gives both the fixed-threshold scores and the change, which flips \padChangedMinPct--\padChangedMaxPct\% of decisions on the real test sets and moves macro-F1 by \padGainMin{} to \padGainMax{} for the fine-tunes. The probes lose a little because their heads were fitted on batch-padded features. The 224 px and no-augmentation ablations had no checkpoint and keep their batch-padded scores, as does the batch-padded fine-tune they are compared with.
